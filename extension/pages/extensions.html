🧩 Missing extensions

Extensions your other browsers have that this browser doesn't.

Set up bookmark sync first: open the Sync Browsers popup and enter your Sync ID and passphrase.

To compare browsers, each browser shares its list of installed extensions (names and store IDs only), encrypted with your passphrase like your bookmarks. It is kept in a separate file from your bookmarks.

Turn this on in each browser you want to compare. Chrome will ask to let Sync Browsers manage your apps, extensions, and themes: it only reads the list and never installs, changes or removes anything.
